Frequently asked questions

Before you start counselling

Can I talk to the AI assistant before sharing my details?

Yes. The website agent can answer approved basic questions. Contact information is captured when needed for counselling or follow-up.

Will the AI decide which college I will get into?

No. In this POC, institute eligibility is calculated from structured criteria. AI only explains the verified result in simple words.

Can I meet an Education Advisor offline?

Yes. The same POC supports walk-in intake. The EA enters minimum information and opens the same counselling workspace.

When is my counselling report sent?

After the draft is generated, the Education Advisor reviews and approves it. Only then is the final PDF sent to the student.
